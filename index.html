Landlord Partnerships

Company Lets & Serviced Accommodation

We partner with landlords seeking responsible, professional corporate arrangements that protect property standards and ensure stress-free day-to-day oversight.

1. Assessing Suitability

We carry out a comprehensive inspection to assess whether your property's location, layout, and configuration are suited to high-quality company lets or professional corporate accommodation.

2. Agreeing Clear Terms

We discuss the intended use frankly and agree upon transparent, formal contractual terms, rent expectations, and operational guidelines before any commitment is made.

3. Approved Furnishing & Setup

We coordinate professional staging, furnishing, and property preparation in strict accordance with pre-agreed specifications and safety standards.

4. Guest Communication, Cleaning & Inspections

We handle vetting, booking communication, round-the-clock guest support, linen logistics, and routine condition inspections to keep your asset in prime order.

5. Maintenance & Handback Responsibilities

Every maintenance workflow, minor repair protocol, and end-of-term handback standard is defined upfront in our agreement so there are never surprises.

Important Clarification for Landlords

Arrangements depend on individual property suitability, requisite permissions, appropriate insurance coverage, and the specific terms of the mutually signed agreement.

Investor Services

Strategic Property Sourcing & Appraisal

We work closely with private investors to evaluate viable residential and corporate opportunities tailored to defined capital and yield parameters.

01
Property Sourcing Against an Agreed Brief

Targeted deal identification according to your budget, desired geographic scope, target returns, and specific strategy criteria.

02
Assessing Projections & Operating Expenses

Detailed models analyzing potential gross income, setup expenditures, ongoing utility costs, cleaning, and maintenance overheads.

03
Coordinating Due Diligence

Verifying local market demand, checking regulatory feasibility, lease restrictions, title details, and compliance requirements.

04
Negotiations & Property Setup Support

Supporting commercial negotiations, landlord/vendor communication, interior fit-outs, and staging to prepare the asset for occupancy.

05
Agreed Management & Regular Reporting

Setting up hands-off management workflows, periodic statements, booking summaries, and ongoing operational maintenance.

Complaints Procedure

At Zorif Properties Ltd, we are committed to providing a high-quality property service to all landlords, investors, and partners. If something does not meet your expectations, we want to know so we can resolve it.

Step 1: Informal & Internal Review

Please write to us with full details at firoz@zorifpropertiesltd.com or by post to 66 Paul Street, London, EC2A 4NA. We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and conduct a thorough internal investigation.

Step 2: Formal Investigation & Final Viewpoint

We will issue a formal written viewpoint within 15 working days of receipt.

Step 3: Property Redress Scheme (PRS) Escalation

If you remain dissatisfied following our final viewpoint, you may refer your grievance to the Property Redress Scheme of which we are a member (Membership Number: PRS061215):
