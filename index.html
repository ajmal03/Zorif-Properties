For landlords

Guaranteed rent & company lets

With guaranteed rent, Zorif Properties rents your property on an agreed term and pays you a fixed monthly rent, even when the property is empty. The rent, the term and who looks after what are all set out in a written agreement before anything starts. We then let it out as a company let or serviced accommodation and look after the day-to-day.

01

Viewing

We view your property and talk through whether it suits a company let or serviced accommodation.

02

Clear terms

We agree the rent, the term and the responsibilities with you in writing.

03

Setup

We furnish and prepare the property to the standard we agreed.

04

Ongoing care

We handle guests, cleaning, regular inspections and the agreed maintenance. At the end of the term, we hand the property back as agreed.

Not every property is suitable. Any arrangement depends on the property, the right permissions (for example from your mortgage lender, freeholder or council), suitable insurance and a signed agreement.
